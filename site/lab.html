Network lab

A workbench for everything on this site: build or load a network and measure it. Nothing is uploaded; pasted data stays in your browser.

  • Generate a Barabási–Albert network and a random network with the same $N$ and $\langle k\rangle$: compare $k_{\max}$, $\langle k^2\rangle$ and $\langle d\rangle$.
  • Watts–Strogatz with $p = 0$, $0.01$ and $1$: watch $\langle C\rangle$ and $\langle d\rangle$.
  • Holme–Kim adds triangles to preferential attachment: how much does $\langle C\rangle$ rise as the triad probability goes from 0 to 1?
  • A configuration model with $\gamma = 2.1$: is its power-law fit close to 2.1? What does the largest component look like?
  • Load the Florentine families: which family has the highest betweenness? (Padgett and Ansell used this network to explain the rise of the Medici.)
