Network lab

Build or load a network and measure it with every tool on this site. Nothing is uploaded; pasted data stays in your browser.

  • Each model predicts a few measures; here you test those predictions side by side.
  • Compare a network with a model of the same $N$ and $\langle k\rangle$ to see what the model explains.

What the measures tell you

$\langle k\rangle$, $\langle k^2\rangle$
density and heterogeneity: a large $\langle k^2\rangle$ means hubs (Ch 4)
$k_{\max}$
largest hub, vs Poisson or natural cutoff (Ch 4)
largest component
share of nodes that can reach each other (Ch 3)
$\langle d\rangle$, diameter
typical and longest shortest path (small world)
$\langle C\rangle$, $C_\Delta$
local and global triangle density, vs random $\langle k\rangle/N$ (Ch 2)
$r$
degree correlation of linked nodes (Ch 7)
power-law fit
maximum-likelihood $\hat\gamma$ and $k_{\min}$ (Ch 4)
$P(K \ge k)$, $C(k)$, $k_{nn}(k)$
tail shape; hierarchy (falling $C(k)$); assortativity
betweenness
share of shortest paths through a node
closeness
inverse mean distance to all others
eigenvector, PageRank
importance from important neighbours
k-core
largest $k$ such that the node survives repeated removal of nodes with fewer than $k$ links
  • BA vs random network, same $N$ and $\langle k\rangle$: compare $k_{\max}$, $\langle k^2\rangle$, $\langle d\rangle$ (BA).
  • Watts–Strogatz at $p = 0$, $0.01$, $1$: $\langle d\rangle$ drops long before $\langle C\rangle$.
  • Holme–Kim, triad probability 0 → 1: how far does $\langle C\rangle$ rise?
  • Configuration model, $\gamma = 2.1$: does the fit return 2.1? Is $r$ negative (structural cutoff, Ch 7)?
  • Random geometric: high $C$ and long $\langle d\rangle$, like the power grid.
  • Florentine families: who has the highest betweenness? (Padgett and Ansell: the Medici.)
